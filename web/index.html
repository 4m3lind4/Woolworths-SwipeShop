Swipe Shop

A clickable prototype of personalised bundles. Start at Personalised Bundles on the home screen, pick a shopper type, open a bundle and tap Swipe shop.

  • Swipe right or tap Buy it to keep a product
  • Swipe left or tap Remove to skip it
  • Swipe up for another option, down to go back

Prototype for a university case competition. Product names, prices and images come from Woolworths' public product search and may be out of date. Bundle photos are openly licensed; see STOCK_CREDITS.md.
